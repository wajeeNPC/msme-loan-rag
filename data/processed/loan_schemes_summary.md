# Summary of MSME related Loan Schemes implemented by the Government

Source: treasury_msme_loan_schemes_summary.pdf (2 pages)

| # | Field | SMELoC | SMELOC2 | Component I (Re-energizing MSME) | Component II (Re-energizing MSME, NPL) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Eligible Categories | Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) | Micro, Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (MSMEs) | MSMEs in operational | MSMEs in operational but classified as Non-performing borrowers |
| 2 | Eligible Sectors | All sectors except trading, leasing and renting businesses | Agriculture, tourism, manufacturing, technology, export-oriented sectors, and women-led MSMEs | Manufacturing, construction, export-oriented, apparel, agriculture and agro-base industries, and women-led MSMEs | MSMEs operational but classified NPL due to unsettled loans from the recent economic downturn (fully closed-down MSMEs excluded) |
| 3 | Types of Loans | Investment, working capital, or combined | Working capital | Investment | Working capital |
| 4 | Source of Fund | Asian Development Bank (ADB) | Asian Development Bank (ADB) | Government of Sri Lanka (GoSL) | Government of Sri Lanka (GoSL) |
| 5 | Eligibility Criteria | (i) Must be registered (or evidence of accepted registration application). (ii) Annual turnover < Rs. 1,000 million AND < 200 employees (group-wide). | Same as SMELoC | Same as SMELoC | Same as Comp I, PLUS: operational but NPL due to unsettled loans from the recent downturn (fully closed-down excluded); priority to those who became NPL as guarantor for others' loans; willful defaulters/fraud/fund diversion excluded |
| 6 | Implementing Licensed Banks | Licensed state and private banks (e.g. Bank of Ceylon, People's Bank, Regional Development Bank, Hatton National Bank, Seylan Bank, Sampath Bank, Commercial Bank of Ceylon, DFCC Bank, National Development Bank, Nations Trust Bank, Sanasa Development Bank, Union Bank of Colombo, Pan Asia Banking Corporation, and others). | 7 | Maximum Loan Amount | Rs. 50.0 million (multiple loans allowed, up to Rs. 50M total) | Rs. 10 million (one loan only) | Rs. 15 million per MSME (one loan only) | Rs. 5 million per MSME (one loan only) |
| 8 | Interest Rate | Max 10% (current); set by PFI based on borrowed rate, admin costs, risk, margin — in line with market rates | Max 8% | Max 7% | Max 8% |
| 9 | Grace Period | Max 2 years | Max 6 months | Max 12 months | Max 6 months |
| 10 | Repayment Period | Generally 10 years incl. grace period, but reduced to max 5 years (per bank-to-government repayment constraints) | Max 3 years incl. grace period | Max 10 years incl. grace period | Max 5 years incl. grace period |
| 11 | Collateral | (i) Working capital loans should be non-collateralized. (ii) PFI obtains suitable collateral for other loans. | Collateral as determined by the PFI | Collateral as determined by the PFI | PFI re-evaluates existing collateralized assets; may require new collateral if insufficient |
